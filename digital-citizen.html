1. What is a digital citizen?

A digital citizen is someone who uses the internet and technology in a responsible and respectful way. Being a good digital citizen means knowing how to communicate online, protect personal information, and make smart choices when using websites, social media, and other digital tools.

🤝

2. How should students behave online?

Students should treat others online the same way they would treat people in person. This means being respectful, avoiding cyberbullying, thinking before posting, and not spreading rumors or false information. Students should also give credit when using someone else's work and avoid sharing private details about themselves or others.

👣

3. What is a digital footprint?

A digital footprint is the record of information a person leaves behind when using the internet. Posts, comments, photos, and even some browsing activity can become part of it. A digital footprint matters because information shared online may be saved, copied, or seen later by other people. Making positive choices helps build a good online reputation.

🛡️

4. Why is digital citizenship important?

Digital citizenship is important because technology is a big part of school, work, and everyday life. Learning to use it correctly helps protect privacy, prevent scams, respect others, and avoid unnecessary problems. It also helps people use the internet to learn, connect, and create opportunities.
