💡 My idea

Problem

Students have disconnected technology lessons and tools that can be difficult to navigate.

Solution

One learning website with an agent that operates its features from typed or spoken commands.

Who benefits?

Students, beginners, and anyone studying digital citizenship or technology.

What is different?

Instead of separate websites, lessons, media, shopping, and controls all work together in one interface.

Objectives: Learn technology, stay safe online, and practice hands-free commands. Future features: secure server-based AI, live approved shopping APIs, saved learning progress, and multilingual voice control. Impact: easier, more accessible digital learning.

Storage comparison

HDD: low cost, slower, shock-sensitive. SATA SSD: faster, affordable. NVMe SSD: fastest of these options, higher performance. USB and SD: portable but easy to lose. Optical discs: offline media but need a drive. Cloud: convenient syncing, dependent on connectivity and provider security.

🔐 Security & Privacy

Passwords

Use long, unique passwords and MFA. Never share or reuse passwords.

Phishing

Watch for urgent messages, suspicious links, and fake sign-in requests.

Malware

Viruses, ransomware, and spyware can damage devices or steal information. Update software and avoid unsafe downloads.

Privacy and AI ethics

Limit personal data sharing. Verify AI outputs for misinformation and bias; respect copyright and intellectual property.

📄 Documentation

Project: Digital Citizen Command Center. A single HTML/CSS/JavaScript application combining earlier coursework and interactive features.

Agent implementation: local rule-based intent detection calls navigation, theme, media, shopping, and educational functions. The chat confirms outcomes; unsupported commands show guidance.

Privacy: No payments or real passwords are collected. Browser speech recognition may use a browser provider's processing service. Voice requires explicit permission and can be stopped.

Limitations: No live AI backend, no real shopping API, and voice/media support varies by browser. Test playback and voice on your published website.
