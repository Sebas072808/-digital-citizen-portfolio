Part 1 · Internet technologies

How the Internet works

The Internet is a network of connected networks. When I open a website, my device sends a request in small pieces called packets. Routers guide them to a server, which sends the website data back. My browser puts the pieces together into the page I see.

Internet Service Providers

An ISP is the company that connects a home or phone to the Internet. It carries my data between my device and other networks. Connection speed, price, and availability depend on the provider and the type of service.

Web browsers

A browser is the app I use to view websites, such as Chrome, Firefox, or Safari. It requests web files and displays the text, images, and interactive features. The address bar also helps me visit a specific web address.

Search engines

A search engine helps me find pages when I do not know their addresses. It discovers and organizes web pages, then ranks results for my search. I still need to check the source, date, and evidence before trusting a result.
