Digital Storage Fundamentals

Digital storage saves information for later use. Unlike temporary RAM, storage generally retains your files when power is off. Choosing storage involves capacity, speed, durability, portability, cost, and security.

🔐 Backups, encryption and security

Follow the 3-2-1 backup rule: three copies of important files, on two types of media, with one copy off-site. Encrypt sensitive files, use strong unique passwords and multifactor authentication, keep software updated, and periodically test restoring your backups.
